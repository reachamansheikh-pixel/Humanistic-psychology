Self-concept = how we see ourselves. Rogers split it in two:

Key Takeaways

Humanism: free will, holism and self-actualisation.
Maslow: needs are met in order, ending in self-actualisation.
Rogers: congruence between actual and ideal self supports well-being.
Unconditional positive regard helps people grow.
